## Tier 4

Tier 4 is derived from Tiers 1–3: two or more Chaos choices grant Chaos; two or more Balance choices, or one of each path, grant Balance; and two or more Order choices grant Order.

    </thead>

        <td><ul><li>Your accuracy is always calculated using your target's weakness.</li></ul></td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td><strong>Splash Zone</strong></td>
        <td><ul><li>Area-of-effect and multi-target attacks deal 30% more damage.</li><li>Area-of-effect abilities deal an additional 5% damage per tile occupied by the target.</li></ul></td>
