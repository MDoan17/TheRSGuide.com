## Overview
For those coming from OSRS, you might expect bossing in RS3 to be roughly on par with difficulty and gear recommendations to OSRS. You might expect Vorkath, GWD1, KQ and other bosses that share both games to have similar vibes in terms of the kill itself and equipment required. This is false.

Damage dealt by these bosses range from far less, to far FAR more. For example, General Graardor is a fairly punishing boss in OSRS. You might be familiar with strategies such as marking tiles to attack with specific weapons, or prayer flicking the minions. In RS3 however, this boss is as basic as it comes. This boss can be easily completed in T70 gear with T70 stats, praying against melee while not moving at all. That being said, it is recommended to check guides before killing any boss, especially with the more modern ones.

We recommend guides from the PVME and have linked them below

Another example of this is vorkath. The fight is similar in name only. Nearly every single mechanic is unique, phases are unique, damage types are unique, and damage output from the boss is fairly extreme. This is NOT the same as OSRS.

There is nothing that really compares in difficulty to Colosseum, Inferno or Awakened DT2 bosses, however the Enrage Bosses (Amascut, Telos, Arch-Glacor and Zamorak) near their maximum get quite close in terms of difficulty. If you desire a challenge, seek out any enrage boss at 4000% or above if possible. Some bosses are impossible to solo. Liberation of Mazcab and Amascut in particular, are literally impossible. Croesus, while simple, is designed to be a group fight and while soloable, is HIGHLY recommended not to do so. Use the PVME and Wiki for more information on these bosses.
